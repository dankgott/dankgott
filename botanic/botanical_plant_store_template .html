Expert Advice

How to Help Your Plants Thrive

Every plant comes with detailed instructions, but these foundational rules ensure long-term vibrancy.

Optimal Sunlight

Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid harsh direct rays that can scorch delicate foliage.

Mindful Watering

Check the top two inches of soil. If dry to the touch, water thoroughly until it drains freely from the pot base.

Stable Climate

Keep your plants away from drafty windows, air conditioner vents, and sudden temperature shifts for best growth.
